    Get books without DRM¶

    BookReader opens EPUB and PDF without DRM. This page explains what DRM is, how to tell whether a book has it, and where to buy or download books that do open.

    What DRM is¶

    DRM (Digital Rights Management) is a lock that some stores put on an ebook. The file is encrypted and only the store's own app (Kindle, Apple Books, Adobe Digital Editions) can open it, with your account. Outside that app the content cannot be read, so BookReader cannot open it either.

    There are three cases:

    Type Examples Opens in BookReader?
    Encrypted DRM Kindle, Apple Books, Adobe DRM (Kobo, Google Play and more) No
    Watermark ("social DRM") Many publishers that sell on their own site Yes: it only carries your name inside
    DRM-free The stores in the list below Yes

    How to tell whether a book has DRM¶

    • Before you buy: check the book's page. Look for "DRM-free" or "watermarked". If it says "Adobe DRM" or "protected", it will not open.
    • If you can only read it in the store's app, it almost certainly has DRM.
    • If you downloaded an .acsm file, that is not the book: it is a ticket to download it with Adobe Digital Editions, and the book it downloads has DRM.
    • Kindle formats (.azw, .azw3, .kfx, .mobi) do not open in BookReader, with or without DRM.

    When you upload a book with DRM, BookReader tells you so instead of trying to open it.

    Where to get them¶

    Technical books¶

    • Manning, The Pragmatic Bookshelf, No Starch Press and Packt: sell their books as DRM-free EPUB and PDF on their own sites.
    • Leanpub: books published by their authors, all DRM-free.
    • ebooks.com: books from many publishers, O'Reilly included. Each book's page says whether it has DRM.
    • Humble Bundle: discounted bundles of DRM-free books.

    General stores¶

    • Kobo: some books say "DRM-free" in their details. Those download as EPUB.
    • Google Play Books: when the publisher allows it, you can export the EPUB or PDF.
    • The publisher's website: many publishers sell DRM-free on their own site even when the big stores sell the same book with DRM. It is worth checking there first.

    Free¶

    • Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg: public-domain classics as EPUB.
    • arXiv and other repositories: research papers as PDF.

    BookReader does not remove DRM

    BookReader does not remove DRM from any book or explain how to do it: in many countries, Spain and the rest of the EU included, getting around copy protection is not allowed. If a book you want only comes with DRM, ask the publisher for a DRM-free edition.
