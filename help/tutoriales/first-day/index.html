    Your first day¶

    Twenty minutes, from nothing to your first card. You do not need to configure anything before you start: the agent comes with a free demo.

    1. Install the app (2 min)¶

    Open BookReader in your browser. On a phone, add it to your home screen: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome → menu → Install app.

    Check: it opens full screen and you see the empty library with Upload your first book.

    2. Add a book (2 min)¶

    Press Upload files and pick an EPUB or PDF without DRM. If you do not have one at hand, any technical PDF you can already open in the browser will do.

    Check: the cover and title show up on the shelf.

    3. Make it yours (3 min)¶

    Open the book and tap Reading settings:

    • Theme Light, Sepia or Dark.
    • Font, size and column width.
    • Mode Pages or Scroll.

    Check: you are comfortable reading without touching anything else for the rest of the session.

    4. Read a chapter (5 min)¶

    Actually read, do not skim. If the book has a table of contents (Contents), jump to the chapter you care about. Notice the time left, at the top.

    Check: progress moves, and if you close the book it reopens where you left it.

    5. Ask about a passage (3 min)¶

    Select a paragraph you did not fully get. In the bar that appears, press Explain this.

    Check: the answer cites the passage and you can tap the citation to go back to the book. If the agent says it cannot find it, try a smaller fragment.

    6. Turn the chapter into cards (3 min)¶

    With the book open, open the agent (✦), go to Studio and ask for Flashcards for that chapter. Review the cards and save them to a deck.

    Check: under Library → Decks the deck appears with today's cards.

    7. Review tomorrow (1 min)¶

    Come back to Decks tomorrow and press Review. Two buttons are enough: Again or Good. The card will return when it should, not when you remember to.

    Check: the next day there is something "due today" and the streak starts counting.

    What you can do now¶

    • Add a book and read it your way.
    • Ask with the book in front of you and check the citation.
    • Turn what you read into review.

    Where to go next¶

    • Set up your key if the demo runs short.
    • Organise your library.
    • Read offline.
