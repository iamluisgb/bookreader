Agent · answers from what BookReader is
How much does it cost?

Everything on this page is free: the reader, the agent, flashcards, review and maps. The agent works from the first minute with a free demo, no key needed. If you use it a lot, plug in your own AI key and pay your provider directly, with no subscription. The only paid plan will be Plus (€8 a month, coming soon).

What formats does it read?

EPUB and PDF. The agent reads both equally well: same chat, same citations, same flashcards.

Can I ask in a different language than the book?

Yes. Read in English and ask in Spanish, or the other way around: the agent understands your question, searches the book and answers in your language, citing the original passage.

Which AI can I use?

Out of the box, a free demo. Then any provider with an OpenAI-compatible API: set the URL, your key and whichever model you want. Without the agent, BookReader is still a complete reader.

Can I open my Kindle books?

Only DRM-free EPUB and PDF. Books bought on Kindle are locked to Amazon’s apps. Many technical publishers sell their books as DRM-free EPUB and PDF.

Do my books get uploaded anywhere?

No. They stay in your browser’s storage. If you turn on sync, they go to your own Google Drive. The agent sends the AI only the text it needs for each answer.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. It runs in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen to use it like an app and read offline.

What if BookReader shuts down?

Everything is on your device and keeps working offline. You can export a full backup, your cards to Anki and your notes to Markdown at any time. And the code is public on GitHub.

Why not just use NotebookLM or Anki?

NotebookLM answers questions about your documents, but it isn’t where you read, and it doesn’t schedule what you should review. Anki schedules review, but you write every card by hand. BookReader is the reader, the agent and the review in one place, and your cards still export to Anki.
